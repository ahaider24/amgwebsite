How long does roofing SEO take to work? +
Map pack movement can start in weeks: a tuned Google Business Profile, steady reviews, and clean local signals move faster than most owners expect. Competitive organic rankings take months, usually three to six before the compounding is obvious. That is why we pair SEO with ads: paid covers the storm that hits next week while SEO builds the position that wins next season.
Should a roofing company buy ads or do SEO? +
Both, honestly. Ads are the only lever that works this week: when a storm hits, Google Ads and LSA put you in front of homeowners immediately, and you turn them off when the surge passes. SEO is slower and it compounds: every month of ranking brings calls you did not pay per click for. Ads buy the surge. SEO owns the season.
How do AI answers change roofing marketing? +
Homeowners now ask ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews who the best roofer near them is, and they get a short list of names instead of a page of links. Those answers pull from your reviews, your entity consistency across the web, and how directly your pages answer real questions. If you are not in the answer, you are not in the running, so we build for that surface from day one.
