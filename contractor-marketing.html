What should a contractor's website actually do? +
Prove you are worth calling back. License and insurance visible, real completed jobs with photos, one page per service so the site matches the search, and fast on a phone. A contractor website is not a brochure; it is the verification step every referral and every bid goes through before anyone returns your call.
How does local SEO for contractors work? +
Google ranks local results on relevance, proximity, and prominence. For contractors that means a complete Google Business Profile in the right categories, steady reviews with real responses, a page for each service that matches what people search, and a consistent name, address, and phone everywhere you appear. The map pack takes most of the clicks, so that is where the work concentrates.
Do AI answers matter for contractors? +
Yes, and more every quarter. Homeowners ask ChatGPT who builds ADUs in their city. Adjusters and GCs read AI summaries when they vet a sub. Those answers pull from your reviews, your entity consistency, and how directly your pages answer the question. We build for that surface from day one, not as an afterthought.
