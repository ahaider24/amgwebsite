# Axiom card exploration

Three directions remain open. The useful choice is between a single painted-edge card that can cross both audiences and a two-card linen set with different voices. My provisional read is **two variants on Natural White linen, Directions 1 and 3**, if texture and handwriting matter most. Direction 2 is the strongest single-card candidate if the physical orange edge matters most. There is no approved final design in this folder.

Start with [the comparison](proofs/comparison-300dpi-1.png). It shows all six faces at their physical trim proportions and native 300-DPI scale. The orange strip under Direction 2 is a schematic edge view outside the card face. It is not a printed border.

All paths in this report are relative to `brand/axiom/`. Every PDF and PNG is an exploration asset, not a printer upload. Artwork studies omit simulated stock. Material studies add a visible paper and texture approximation. Never print the simulated linen pattern.

## The material decision

Axiom lists 100# Natural White and Solar White linen with an embossed crosshatch surface and writing suitability. Natural White is the appropriate warm starting point here. It is not guaranteed to equal brand paper `#FBF8F2`. The proof uses that brand colour as a visual proxy. No pale background flood is present in the linen artwork studies. [Axiom linen product](https://axiomprint.com/product/linen-business-cards-764).

The painted-edge product uses ultra-thick 28PT or 32PT stock with a silky matte face. Custom edge matching is offered. Request `#FF4D1C` as the initial colour reference, then approve a physical paint sample. Hex is not a paint specification. The smooth proof ground is a neutral warm placeholder, not a claim about the actual sheet colour. [Axiom painted-edge product](https://axiomprint.com/product/painted-edge-cards-425).

These are separate product systems. The off-the-shelf linen card does not offer painted edges. A linen plus painted-edge hybrid remains an unconfirmed custom request. No proof disguises that hybrid as an available product, and no price, minimum quantity or custom turnaround is assumed.

## Direction 1: Natural linen, a working card

Landscape, 3.5 x 2 in trim. Stacked Geist wordmark, a prominent phone number, one tiny orange square, and a simple contact block. Amir's name anchors the right side without competing with the wordmark. The reverse carries the exact existing site line and the service locations. The central reverse remains bare, writable stock: a reserved 3.0 x 0.75 in rectangle from x 0.25 to 3.25 in and y 0.80 to 1.55 in, measured from trim top left.

The restraint comes from allowing the linen to supply the surface character. There is no full dark flood, engraving, glow, foil or decorative rule. The single orange square is 3pt, about 0.042 in. It is a colour accent, not information that must be read.

**Hedge fund verdict: not my choice as shown.** The front is credible and readable, but the reverse's "Get found. Get clients. Get back to life." addresses an owner-operator's daily burden. In a principal introduction it can feel like a small-business acquisition pitch. The texture cannot solve that mismatch. Remove the slogan for that audience, give Amir's name more priority, and carry the brand with typography alone. Direction 3 tests those changes.

**Contractor verdict:** the most useful of the three for a quick handoff, a phone call and a handwritten reminder. Phone is 11.5pt, email and URL are 10pt, and contact information is all on one face. All type is dark on light paper.

## Direction 2: Painted edge, typographic confidence

Landscape, 3.5 x 2 in trim. The front is a large, centered ink wordmark. The familiar italic treatment of Leads keeps the creative-agency identity. The reverse is Amir and three contact lines, with a 12pt phone number and 10.5pt email and URL. No slogan, no illustration, no orange on either face. The edge alone supplies orange.

The comparison includes a 32PT schematic edge, drawn 2.304pt deep at physical scale. That is one of the listed thickness options, not a selected order or a measured finished sample. A flat face proof cannot communicate the paint texture, heft or finish. The 28PT option remains open.

**Hedge fund verdict: yes, with a physical-sample condition.** The two faces show confidence through scale, spacing and clear contact details. They retain the actual agency name rather than trying to resemble a private bank. The vivid edge is the risk: a clean, restrained application can carry it; a glossy, uneven or visually loud edge could undermine it. Approve the physical orange on the actual stock. If it overwhelms the object, choose Direction 3 rather than adding decorative finishes to rescue it.

**Contractor verdict:** clear and legible, but the reader must turn it over for contact information. Writable performance on the silky matte face is unconfirmed, so this direction does not promise a useful note surface.

## Direction 3: Personal linen, a quiet introduction

Portrait reading orientation, 2 x 3.5 in trim, using the same physical standard card and proposed Natural White linen as Direction 1. Amir Haider is the primary identity. The agency name appears directly beneath him, so restraint does not hide his business. Contact information stays together on the front. The reverse has only the established stacked wordmark and generous bare paper. A reserved 1.5 x 1.9 in note rectangle runs from x 0.25 to 1.75 in and y 1.0 to 2.9 in.

This direction fills the gap left by the others: a personal introduction whose premium signal comes from paper and editorial judgment, without relying on thickness or coloured paint. No orange and no slogan. The wordmark's existing italic Leads preserves some motion and recognizability. The portrait is deliberate, but still fits a standard card holder.

**Hedge fund verdict: strongest of the three for a conservative principal introduction.** It asks the person to remember Amir, then the agency. It does not imply financial credentials, invent a role or add an institutional-sounding claim. The caveat is physical: 100# linen should not be sold to ourselves as having the heft of a 32PT card. Its quality must be judged in hand.

**Contractor verdict:** usable, but weaker than Direction 1 in a truck. Email is 8.5pt and location is 8pt to fit the narrow measure, versus 10pt email on Direction 1. If this becomes the only card, test those lines at actual size and in ordinary light. A landscape translation with larger contact type would be the next useful variant, not extra ornament.

## One card or two

**My read: two voices are justified.** Carry Direction 1 for owner-operator conversations and Direction 3 for quieter introductions. Both use the same proposed linen, same ink-led identity and same physical size. This is a meaningful audience distinction, not an arbitrary luxury tier. Ask Axiom to quote both artworks in a combined order; do not assume mixed artwork counts as a single economical run. The incremental price and minimums are unconfirmed.

If Amir wants one card and a painted edge, Direction 2 is the credible compromise. It serves both rooms without the slogan, but trades away confirmed writability and the linen feel he originally named. Keep all three open until the stock samples and these proofs are compared at actual size.

## Linen test and visual inspection

[Stress-test PDF](linen-stress-test.pdf) and [300-DPI stress-test proof](proofs/linen-stress-test-300dpi-1.png) place three equal-scale controls next to simulated linen: sparse lettering, a full dark flood with reversed lettering, and the supplied spark engraving. All panels are 3.5 x 2 in. The source spark is unchanged and cropped geometrically in the PDF. At 3.5 in wide its 1024 pixels provide about 292.6 effective DPI; rendering at 300 DPI does not create more source detail. It is used for testing, not in any proposed card face.

The crosshatch relief is procedural vector shading. The stress column also adds an explicitly assumed faint defect field. Thread spacing, relief, shade and ink loss are not measured from Axiom stock. This is a sensitivity visualization, not experimental evidence that Axiom's press will fail. The control and stress column must be considered together. A physical sample with these exact panels is the actual printing test.

I visually inspected all twelve individual face renders: both pages of each artwork PDF and both pages of each material PDF. I also inspected the comparison and stress-test sheets, then re-rendered and inspected the changed linen views after refining the relief simulation. The final crosshatch is visible in the white space and does not interfere with the proposed card lettering. Counters, phone punctuation and the email @ remain distinct. No card text is clipped, overlapping or outside the working safe area. Direction 3's narrow email line fits, but its 8.5pt size remains the weakest readability choice at actual card size.

In the stress test, sparse positive type remains easy to read with the assumed texture and defects over it. The large reversed name also survives, so this does not support a blanket claim that all reversed type fails on linen. The dark flood becomes visibly uneven and busy, while the engraved fingers acquire competing crosshatching that makes their finest hatching harder to separate. The fingertip gap and main silhouettes remain recognizable. This supports keeping large solid fields and the spark off these linen concepts as a design precaution. It does not establish an actual Axiom mottle rate or prove that its press will break those lines. Those outcomes remain unconfirmed until a physical print test.

The smooth Direction 2 has the clearest separation of typography and material. Its face proofs contain no orange; orange appears only in the labeled external edge schematic. The comparison has been checked for readable labels, consistent physical card scale and uncropped panels. A screen viewer may resize the sheet, so judge physical type size by printing the comparison PDF at 100 percent with no fit-to-page scaling. The comparison sheet is 8.75 x 13 in and needs suitable paper; individual card PDFs can be printed at actual size on a larger sheet.

## Working geometry and file status

- Working trim: 3.5 x 2 in, or the same card read portrait at 2 x 3.5 in.
- Working bleed: 0.125 in per edge. Landscape artboard 3.75 x 2.25 in, 270 x 162pt. Portrait artboard 2.25 x 3.75 in, 162 x 270pt.
- Working safety: 0.125 in inside trim. All glyph bounds are programmatically checked against it. Most content sits further in.
- These values are a standard US convention chosen for exploration, not Axiom-confirmed specifications. Obtain the actual product template before press preparation.
- Individual face proofs: 1125 x 675px landscape and 675 x 1125px portrait, including bleed, rendered directly from each PDF at 300 DPI.
- Six two-page PDFs: three bare artwork studies and three material studies. Front is page 1, back page 2. No crop marks. Portrait pages remain upright; confirm upload rotation and duplex flip with Axiom.
- Comparison PDF and stress-test PDF each have one page. The comparison uses actual-size trim views, not bleed views.
- Real Geist 700 outlines and existing Archivo 400/600 outlines are used. Geist italic Leads uses the established 14-degree synthetic slant. Card type is vector geometry, not a substituted system font. Review-sheet labels use Helvetica.
- Colour is exploratory DeviceRGB. No Axiom CMYK profile, spot-ink recipe, output intent, total ink limit or PDF/X conformance is asserted. `#14110A` is the visual ink reference, not an approved black separation. Ask prepress about single-black small type and the orange accent.
- Stock tint and texture appear only in material studies. The underlying linen artwork contains no background flood. Painted-edge colour is an external finishing request, absent from the face artwork PDFs.

## Open questions for Axiom before printing

1. **Exact templates:** send the current templates for 100# Natural White linen and 28PT/32PT painted-edge cards. Confirm trim, bleed, safe area, trim tolerance, page boxes, marks and duplex orientation, including the portrait variant.
2. **File delivery:** accepted file types, separate fronts/backs versus two-page PDF, outline or embedded font preference, minimum raster resolution, PDF/X requirement and any upload rotation rules.
3. **Colour preparation:** required colour mode and ICC profile, rendering intent, black recipe for small type, overprint and trapping policy, total ink limit, and whether an ink that reads near `#14110A` is practical without registration risks. Confirm how the small orange accent should be specified.
4. **Linen sample:** current Natural White manufacturer and exact stock, physical sheet shade, texture scale and direction, finished thickness, stock availability, and a printed sample of 8 to 12pt type. Is both-side texture consistent? What print process would be used for this quantity?
5. **Linen limitations:** minimum positive and reversed line weights, treatment of dense dark solids and fine engraving, and whether a physical proof using the stress-test panels can be supplied. The digital simulation cannot answer these.
6. **Writing:** verify that the supplied linen finish remains uncoated and suitable for pen and pencil after printing. Test the chosen pen on the actual stock. Is the silky matte painted-edge face writable at all, with which pens and drying time?
7. **Painted-edge build:** available 28PT and 32PT constructions, face colour and finish, exact final thickness, corner choices and whether paint can creep onto faces, scuff or transfer in a wallet. Request a finished sample.
8. **Custom orange:** how should `#FF4D1C` be translated into a physical paint reference? Is a drawdown or finished edge sample available? Confirm sheen, colour tolerance, sample approval process and repeat-order consistency. No exact screen-to-paint match is assumed.
9. **Linen plus painted edge:** can Axiom make it as a custom product while retaining genuine exposed linen and writability? What construction and thickness are required? Would bonding, sealing or finishing alter texture or the writing surface? Obtain feasibility, samples and a written quote before treating it as an option.
10. **Two variants in one order:** can Directions 1 and 3 share stock procurement and production despite different art and reading orientation? Confirm per-variant minimums, setup charges, proof charges, quantity splits, collation and incremental cost. No price claim is made here.
11. **Schedule and approval:** confirm current production timing for the chosen stock, custom colour and proofing, plus delivery. The brief's listed standard times are not a quote for this job. Confirm the point at which art and physical colour approval lock the order, and the remedy for unacceptable trim or edge finish.

## Reproduction and scope

Built on `feat/brand-assets`, entirely under `brand/axiom/`. Existing Moo outputs, source fonts, avatars and site files are preserved. No HTML, hosting configuration, push, deployment or print order is part of this exploration.

No caller-provided command-line `-o` destination was visible in the request. I asked for it and used the announced default:

```sh
/Users/amir/.cache/codex-runtimes/codex-primary-runtime/dependencies/python/bin/python3 brand/axiom/build.py -o brand/axiom/report.md
```

The builder copies this report source to the supplied `-o` path and records that destination, input hashes, PDF geometry and glyph safety bounds in [verification.json](verification.json). All newly authored text is checked for the forbidden em dash.
