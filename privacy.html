Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Amir Gets LLC, doing business as AmirGetsJobs ("AMG," "we," "us"), based in Fresno, California, respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect through amirgetsjobs.com and our services, how we use it, and your rights, including your rights under California law.

1. Information We Collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email, phone number, company, and details about your business when you contact us, book a call, or purchase a service.
  • Payment information: processed securely by our payment provider, Stripe. We do not receive or store your full card number.
  • Scheduling: if you book a call, our scheduling provider (Calendly) processes the details you enter.
  • Usage data: we use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used (pages viewed, device and browser type, approximate region). This is collected through cookies and similar technology.

2. How We Use It

  • To respond to you, schedule calls, and deliver the services you request.
  • To process payments and issue any refunds.
  • To understand and improve site performance and our services.

3. How We Share Information

We share information only with service providers who help us operate, such as Stripe (payments), Calendly (scheduling), Google Analytics (usage measurement), and our hosting provider, each acting on our behalf. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising or with third parties for their own direct marketing. We may disclose information if required by law.

4. Cookies, Analytics, and Do Not Track

The site uses cookies needed for basic functionality and Google Analytics cookies to measure usage. You can disable cookies in your browser, and you can opt out of Google Analytics using Google's browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. Because browser "Do Not Track" signals are not handled consistently across the web, we do not currently respond to them; the choices described here are how you control tracking on our site.

5. Data Retention and Security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, our records, and legal obligations, then delete or anonymize it. We use reasonable safeguards to protect it, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Your California Privacy Rights

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it, to access or receive a copy of it, to request correction or deletion, to opt out of any sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell or share it), and to not be treated differently for exercising these rights. Under California's "Shine the Light" law (Civil Code section 1798.83), we confirm we do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing. To make a request, call (559) 550-5474 or reach us through the site; we will verify and respond as the law requires.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the current version.

8. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Call (559) 550-5474 or reach us through amirgetsjobs.com.
