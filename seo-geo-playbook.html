What's the difference between SEO and GEO? +
SEO is search engine optimization: getting you to rank on Google, in the map pack and the links. GEO is generative engine optimization: getting you named inside the AI answer when someone asks ChatGPT, Google's AI Overview, or Perplexity. Different surfaces, heavily overlapping work, and you need both now.
If AI is taking over, why still do SEO at all? +
Two reasons. Plenty of buyers, especially in home service, still use the map and the links the old way. And the AI itself leans on Google's signals, your profile, reviews, and rankings, to decide who's trustworthy enough to name. Strong SEO is part of how you win GEO. Dropping it would hurt both.
How long until I see results? +
The profile and citation fixes can move the map in weeks. Ranking pages and earning AI citations is more of a months-long build, because trust accumulates. We front-load the fast wins so you feel momentum early while the compounding work runs underneath.
Can I just do this myself? +
You can, the same way you could do your own books. The plays aren't secret, they're just constant. Most owners don't have the hours every week to claim listings, chase reviews, tune pages, and publish content while running the actual business. That weekly grind is what you're hiring out.
Do I own all of it if we part ways? +
Yes. The profile, the content, the site, all of it lives on your accounts and stays yours. No lock-in, nothing held hostage.
