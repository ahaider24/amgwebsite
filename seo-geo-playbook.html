Why it's both now

One front door became two. You have to own both.

SEO still matters. People still pull up the map, still tap the top three, still read reviews before they call. If you're not ranking locally, you're invisible to everyone who searches the old way, and there are still plenty of them.

But a growing share asks the AI instead, and it answers with a couple of names. Getting named there is GEO, and it's a different discipline. The good news: the AI decides who to trust using a lot of the same signals as Google, so the work overlaps more than it competes.

Run only SEO and you win the link nobody clicks. Run only GEO and you're missing the buyers still on the map. Run both and you show up wherever the customer looks. That's the whole playbook.

The plays we run

The same foundation feeds the map and the machine.

None of this is a secret formula. It's disciplined fundamentals, done consistently, in the order that compounds. Here's the shape of it, kept high-level on purpose.

01
the foundation · SEO

Own your Google Business Profile

The single biggest lever for a local business. Claimed, complete, categorized right, posted to regularly, with the photos and hours and service area all correct. It's what the map ranks on, and it's one of the first things the AI checks to decide if you're real.

The base every other play stands on.
02

Reviews, citations, and consistency

A steady flow of real reviews, your name, address, and phone identical everywhere they appear, and listings that all agree. This is the trust layer. Google ranks it and the AI reads it as evidence you're legit before it dares put your name in an answer.

The trust signals both engines reward.
03
the site · SEO

Pages that rank and load

Fast, clean pages for the services and towns you actually serve, structured so search understands exactly what you do and where. The unglamorous on-page work that decides whether you sit in the pack or on page two.

Found for the searches that turn into calls.
04
the quotable layer · GEO

Content an AI can confidently quote

Clear, specific answers to the real questions in your trade, written so a model can lift a sentence and trust it. Straight answers, real expertise, structured cleanly. This is what turns you from a link into a named source in the answer.

You become the thing AI cites, not skips.
05
the scoreboard · both

Measure rank and citations, not vanity

We track where you sit on the map, whether the AI names you for your core questions, and whether any of it turns into a call. We adjust to what's moving the number that matters, which is revenue, not impressions.

Straight answers

SEO and GEO, answered.

SEO is search engine optimization: getting you to rank on Google, in the map pack and the links. GEO is generative engine optimization: getting you named inside the AI answer when someone asks ChatGPT, Google's AI Overview, or Perplexity. Different surfaces, heavily overlapping work, and you need both now.

Two reasons. Plenty of buyers, especially in home service, still use the map and the links the old way. And the AI itself leans on Google's signals, your profile, reviews, and rankings, to decide who's trustworthy enough to name. Strong SEO is part of how you win GEO. Dropping it would hurt both.

The profile and citation fixes can move the map in weeks. Ranking pages and earning AI citations is more of a months-long build, because trust accumulates. We front-load the fast wins so you feel momentum early while the compounding work runs underneath.
