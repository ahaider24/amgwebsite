# Resolution

## Research

Inspected eight live homepage OG assets, archived in `research.json`. [Instrument](https://www.instrument.com/) combines an oversized identity with cropped work. [COLLINS](https://wearecollins.com/), [BUCK](https://buck.co/), [Koto](https://koto.studio/) and [Linear](https://linear.app/) prioritize instantly recognizable identity. [Vercel](https://vercel.com/) isolates a dimensional symbol. [Active Theory](https://activetheory.net/) supplies immersive material complexity. [Raycast](https://www.raycast.com/) pairs one large promise with product imagery. These are observations of the fetched cards, not claims about conversion performance.

## Five principles

1. One dominant gesture must survive reduction to 300 pixels wide.
2. Large silhouettes establish recognition; fine detail rewards closer inspection.
3. Separate type from visual complexity with real negative space.
4. Limit color so one deliberate accent carries attention.
5. Give the viewer one clear idea, with a legible identity and destination.

## Art direction

An optical sculpture turns many graphite filaments into one concentrated cobalt ribbon: the moment a business becomes distinguishable within AI discovery. Monumental glass, fine silver lines and soft directional daylight give the image physical credibility. Paper ivory #FBF8F2 and ink #14110A anchor the brand; ultramarine supplies the only saturated color. Precise Geist typography occupies the quiet left field, while Fraunces italic appears only in Jobs. The image expresses clarity and recognition without depicting an AI interface or promising guaranteed rankings.

## Generation prompt

One built-in image generation call, 1536x1024. The exact complete prompt is preserved in [image-prompt.txt](image-prompt.txt). Its core instruction:

> A very large thick circular optical-glass lens, standing almost upright at a slight oblique angle, fills the RIGHT HALF of the frame. Behind and around it, hundreds of exquisitely fine graphite and silver filaments sweep in a broad curved fan, like brushed metal silk or a physical moire sculpture. Within the glass the fine scattered lines resolve into a single striking smooth deep ultramarine cobalt ribbon, making a sculptural fluid loop through the lens.

Additional constraints specified empty left space, ivory daylight, restrained materials, no orange or red, and no text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks. The selected output is `still.png`.

**og-alt.png:** Same sculpture, a larger four-line treatment of “Get found. Get clients. Get back to life.”
