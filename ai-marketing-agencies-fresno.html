How this list was built

  1. What each agency says it does, taken from its own website, not paraphrased into puffery.
  2. Who the AI assistants recommend. In July 2026 we asked Google's AI Mode which AI agencies serve Fresno and recorded exactly who it named. That test is repeated monthly.
  3. Public review presence, counted only where the agency's Google Business Profile could be confidently matched. Where it couldn't, we say so instead of guessing.
  4. Focus: marketing-first shops are listed separately from AI-buildout shops, because hiring one when you need the other is the most common mistake we see.
