Built by an operator, for operators.

AmirGets is an AI-native marketing and SEO agency for home service businesses. It's built by someone who spent a decade shipping these exact systems at scale, then brought them home to Fresno to do it for the people he grew up around.

The operator

Trained at the highest level. Home to do it for his own.

From the millions of text messages that ping your phone every time you pick up your Mercedes, to delivering car repair to your driveway, to solving prediction markets and content aggregation before most people had heard of Kalshi or knew what a feed was: Amir spent his career on the cutting edge of tech and marketing, building for some of the biggest names in the business.

Now he's back in Fresno, doing it for the people he grew up around. The contractors, the family businesses, the shops that have always run on word of mouth. The lead response, the instant quote, the follow-up that never drops a customer, the same systems, brought home to the people who actually need them.

Same story every time, a great business quietly leaking customers between the lead and the close. Sealing that gap is the whole job. The only difference now is whose business it is.
