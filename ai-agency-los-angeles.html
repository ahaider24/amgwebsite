01 · THE CITY

Los Angeles raised me.

Which is why it crushes my soul to watch what the city has become for the people trying to run a small business in it.

02 · WHAT I WATCHED

I saw it happen, spot by spot.

The Standard rooftop downtown, where USC kids went after an exam, gone. Add it up and the lesson is brutal: in this town, the margin for error in a small business is minute. You cannot afford an inexperienced marketer, and you cannot afford to be slow with an estimate. Ever.
